    Structured Drug-Drug Interaction Safety Checker

    Deterministic lookup against verified clinical interaction labeling (FDA DailyMed / RxNorm). Never delegates drug safety reasoning to ungrounded heuristics.

    Clinical Safety Architecture & Governance

    Comprehensive guardrails governing Phase 5 OCR inference and Phase 6 Knowledge RAG.

    🚨 Emergency Short-Circuiting

    Acute life-threatening symptoms (e.g. crushing chest pain, anaphylaxis, severe dyspnea, acute poisoning/overdose) are classified at the pre-generation safety layer and immediately routed to emergency dispatch directives (911 / 112 / 999) rather than attempting clinical Q&A.

    🔍 Zero-Guessing Verification Policy

    Prescription handwriting marked review_required or unverified is never silently converted into a likely medicine. Uncertain text is explicitly flagged for pharmacist verification.

    💊 Structured Drug Interactions

    Drug interaction checks query deterministic clinical rules from DailyMed package inserts. If an interaction is absent from the verified database, it is explicitly reported as unverified rather than invented.

    🔒 Patient Privacy & Ephemeral Data

    Prescription images are processed in-memory and immediately discarded. No patient names, images, or PHI are persisted in chat logs or analytics.
